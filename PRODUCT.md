# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Event planners, hosts, and organizers in Tegucigalpa planning chic social events at private homes and curated venues — weddings, bridal showers, baptisms, baby showers, and birthdays. They are building moments that photograph beautifully and feel elevated. They want their guests to notice the details: the harmony of the tablescape, the quality of the linen, the refinement of the glassware. Instagram is their reference board and their measuring stick.

They are not browsing for inspiration — they have a vision, a date, and a guest list. They need to trust that Savá will make their event look exactly as they imagined.

## Product Purpose

Savá Rentals rents event tableware, linens, and glassware in Tegucigalpa. The website exists to convert a browsing visitor into a WhatsApp inquiry. Success means: visitor lands, browses the catalogue or packages, finds what they need, and taps "Cotizar." No account, no form, no friction — just the product and a direct line to the business.

## Brand Personality

Elegante. Chic. Old money.

Tone: formal yet warm — authoritative without coldness. The brand speaks like a refined host who has done this a hundred times and takes quiet pride in every detail. It does not shout. It does not over-explain. It presents with confidence and lets the work speak.

Tagline: "Elegancia para cada evento."

## Anti-references

- Anything boring, generic, or interchangeable. This should never feel like it could belong to any rental company anywhere.
- Anything that evokes poor quality, damage, or carelessness. Every visual element must signal precision and care.
- Tacky, loud, or garish aesthetics — no visual noise, no neon, no clutter that reads as unsophisticated (naco).
- Cold catalog layouts that feel like a warehouse, not a boutique.

## Design Principles

1. **WhatsApp-first** — every meaningful CTA leads to WhatsApp. The path from "I love this" to "I sent a message" must be frictionless and obvious on mobile.
2. **Excellence in every detail** — no element is accidental. Spacing, typography, color: each choice signals the same care Savá takes with every event it touches.
3. **Old money restraint** — the palette is sober. The layout breathes. Nothing competes for attention. Elegance is earned through discipline, not decoration.
4. **Spanish throughout** — every word on every surface is Spanish. Copy is formal yet warm and direct — never bureaucratic, never stiff. It should feel like it came from a person who takes pride in what they do.
5. **Instagrammable by design** — the catalogue presents products as they would appear on a beautifully styled table. The aesthetic gives visitors confidence that the real event will look this good.
6. **Exclusivity without exclusion** — the visual register signals high-end without intimidating. Aspirational and approachable: every budget should feel welcome to inquire.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA baseline. Mobile-first at 375px, 768px, 1280px. No specific accommodations beyond standard contrast and tap-target sizing.
